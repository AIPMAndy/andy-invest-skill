## 🎯 What This Is

Andy Investment Skill is not a stock recommendation bot. It's a systematic investment framework that helps you:

- **Track & Analyze**: Record trades, attribute returns, analyze performance
- **Plan & Execute**: Build long-term allocation and short-term trading discipline
- **Learn & Improve**: Review decisions, identify patterns, control risks

**这不是荐股机器人**，而是帮你建立投资纪律、风险意识和复盘能力的决策支持系统。

## 🎓 Philosophy & Principles

### Investment, Not Speculation

- **Research-driven**: Understand what you buy
- **Risk-first**: Protect capital before chasing returns
- **Discipline-based**: Follow system, not emotion
- **Review-focused**: Learn from every trade

### AI as Assistant, Human as Decision-maker

- AI helps gather data, identify patterns, check discipline
- Human makes final judgment on allocation, entry, exit
- System enforces rules, prevents emotional trading

**AI 是助手，人是决策者** - 系统帮你收集信息、检查纪律、复盘改进，但最终决策权在你手中
